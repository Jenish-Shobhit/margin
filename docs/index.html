A shelf that waits for you.

Shots stack at the right edge of your screen and stay there, even after a restart, until you put them to use.

  • Drag it anywhere

    Into Finder, Slack, Chrome or Terminal. The card leaves the shelf once the drop lands.

  • Click to copy it again

    Press Space to look closer, or double-click to open. ⇧⌘1 tucks the shelf away.

  • Nothing is ever lost

    ⌘Z brings back the last 20 removals, even files you put in the Trash.

Pixel-perfect when it matters.

Most shots are a quick drag. For the rest, everything you need is on the overlay before you let go.

  • Resize handles, and arrow keys that nudge by 1 px
  • Edges that snap to the windows underneath
  • Z for a magnifier with the exact position and colour
  • Every display at its own Retina scale and colour profile

One window, clean corners.

Press Space and click a window. You get just that window, with transparent rounded corners and nothing behind it.

  • Hold ⌥ to keep its shadow
  • ⇧⌘2 takes the same area again, with no overlay

A real Mac app, and a light one.

Written in Swift for macOS 26. It lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon, no account and no third-party code.

  • Settings laid out like System Settings
  • Light and dark, Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency
  • Updates itself, checked against Margin's own signature
  • Hide the menu bar icon and it keeps working

And all the little things.

  • Never in its own shots

    The shelf, the overlay and the fly-in are left out of every capture and recording.

  • Close the shelf, keep the files

    One click clears every card. The files stay in your folder.

  • Or don't keep them at all

    Turn on Delete screenshots after pasting, and a shot goes once it's done its job.

  • Follows your files

    Rename or move a shot in Finder and its card keeps up.

  • Every Space, every display

    Over full-screen apps too, and it never takes focus from the app you're in.

  • Findable like any screenshot

    Tagged as a screenshot, so Finder and Spotlight find it, with its colour profile kept.

No accounts. No analytics.

Margin works entirely on your Mac. Your shots never leave it unless you drag them somewhere.

  • Only talks to GitHub

    A daily check for a new version, and the download if you choose to install it. Nothing about you is sent. Turn it off in Settings.

  • Only what it needs

    Screen Recording, and the microphone only if you record it. No Accessibility or Input Monitoring.

  • Open source

    MIT licensed, with every line on GitHub.

Everything Margin asks for
Screen Recording
To capture and record the screen. It asks only when you click Allow… in the welcome window.
Your Desktop folder
macOS asks once, before Margin first saves into ~/Desktop/Screenshots. Pick another folder in Settings if you like.
Screenshot shortcuts
macOS keeps ⇧⌘3, ⇧⌘4 and ⇧⌘5 until they're turned off. Turn off for me does that when you click it, and Settings can restore them.
The microphone
Only if you turn on Record microphone for screen recordings.
Launch at login
On by default, so the shortcuts work after a restart. Turn it off in Settings.

Install in a minute.

Free, for macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon, from an account that can install apps.

One line in Terminal Recommended

It downloads the latest release from GitHub, checks its size and checksum against GitHub's and its signing certificate against Margin's, copies it to Applications and opens it. If a check fails, nothing is installed.

$ curl -fsSL https://jenish-shobhit.github.io/margin/install.sh | bash

No sudo and no Open Anyway step; Margin then asks for Screen Recording itself. Read the script first if you like: install.sh.

Or download it

Margin isn't notarized by Apple yet, so a downloaded copy's first launch takes one extra step, once.

How to open it the first time
  1. Unzip the zip and move Margin.app to Applications. Keep it there: its Screen Recording permission belongs to that copy.
  2. Open it. macOS blocks the first launch:
    1. In the "Margin" Not Opened dialog, click Done, not Move to Trash.
    2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
    3. Next to "Margin" was blocked, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  3. Follow the welcome window: allow Screen Recording, hand over the screenshot shortcuts and take your first shot.

Already have Margin? Update Margin, or choose Check for Updates… in its menu. Updates need no Open Anyway.

How to uninstall
  1. Give the screenshot keys back to macOS first: in Margin's Settings › Shortcuts, click Restore under macOS Screenshot Shortcuts.
  2. Turn off Launch at login in Settings › General, then choose Quit Margin from its menu bar icon.
  3. Drag Margin.app from Applications to the Trash. Your screenshots stay in your folder. To remove its settings too, see the full steps.
