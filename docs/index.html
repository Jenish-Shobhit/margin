Margin for Mac

Screenshots that wait in the margin of your screen

Margin saves every capture, copies it, and keeps it on a shelf at the edge of your screen until you drag it where it belongs.

Download Margin Already have it? Update Margin

For macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Free and open source.

Or paste one line into Terminal: Margin installs and opens with no security prompts.

$ curl -fsSL https://jenish-shobhit.github.io/margin/install.sh | bash

Out of your way until you need it.

No preview to dismiss, no save dialog, no editor in between. Margin does the boring part instantly and keeps the shot within reach.

  • Saved and copied, every time

    Each shot is a PNG in ~/Desktop/Screenshots and on the clipboard the moment you let go.

  • A shelf that waits

    Thumbnails stack at the right edge and stay until you drag them into an app, even after a restart.

  • Instant, on a frozen screen

    ⇧⌘4 freezes the screen at once. Letting go of the mouse captures, with no confirm step.

  • ⌘V works in terminals

    The clipboard holds the image and the file, so Ghostty pastes the path and Claude Code attaches the shot.

  • Screen recording, too

    ⇧⌘5 records an area, a window or a display as a Retina MP4 at up to 60 fps, with sound if you want it.

  • Never in its own shots

    The shelf, the selection and the fly-in are left out of every capture and every recording.

  • Close the shelf, keep the files

    Close Shelf clears every card at once. ⌘Z brings back the last 20 removals, even files you put in the Trash.

  • Or don't keep them at all

    Turn on Delete screenshots after pasting, and a shot is gone once it's pasted or dropped where it belongs.

  • Native and light

    Written in Swift for macOS 26. A menu bar icon, no Dock icon, no accounts and no third-party code.

Precise when it matters.

Most shots are a quick drag. For the others, everything you need is on the overlay before you let go.

  • Resize handles and arrow-key nudging, 1 px at a time
  • Edges that snap to the windows underneath
  • Press Z for a magnifier with the exact position and colour
  • Press Space for one window, with clean rounded corners
  • Every display, at its own Retina scale and colour profile

Your shots, in reach.

Drag a card into Finder, Slack, Chrome or Terminal. Click to copy it again, press Space to look closer, and tuck the shelf away with ⇧⌘1.

The shortcuts you already know.

Margin takes over the macOS screenshot keys, with your permission, and you can change any of them in Settings.

  • ⇧⌘4
    Capture an areaPress Space for a single window.
  • ⇧⌘3
    Capture the screenThe display under the pointer.
  • ⇧⌘2
    Repeat last areaThe same area again, no overlay.
  • ⇧⌘5
    Record the screenPress it again to stop.
  • ⇧⌘1
    Tuck the shelfInto a small tab, and back.

Every shot is already copied, so the ⌃ versions do the same. The full list covers the keys for selecting and for the shelf.

Install in a minute.

One line in Terminal is quickest. The same line updates Margin later.

One line in TerminalRecommended

Open Terminal (in Applications › Utilities), paste this and press Return. It downloads the latest release from GitHub, checks its checksum and that it's signed with Margin's certificate, moves it to Applications and opens it. Nothing to approve in System Settings.

$ curl -fsSL https://jenish-shobhit.github.io/margin/install.sh | bash

Read it first if you like: install.sh.

Or download the zip

Margin isn't notarized by Apple yet, so a downloaded copy's first launch takes one extra step, once.

  1. Download and unzip

    Open the zip from your Downloads folder. You get Margin.app.

  2. Move it to Applications

    And keep it there: macOS ties Margin's Screen Recording permission to that copy.

  3. Open it, then Open Anyway

    macOS blocks the first launch of an app that isn't notarized:

    1. In the "Margin" Not Opened dialog, click Done, not Move to Trash.
    2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
    3. Next to "Margin" was blocked, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

  4. Follow the welcome window

    Allow Screen Recording, hand over the screenshot shortcuts, and take your first shot.

Already have Margin?

This opens Margin and checks for a newer version, or choose Check for Updates… in Margin's menu. Nothing is installed until you click Install in Margin's window, and updates need no Open Anyway.

Update Margin

A copy too old to have that menu item needs a fresh download instead.

Mac

Price

No accounts. No analytics.

Margin works entirely on your Mac and sends no telemetry. Your screenshots and recordings never leave it unless you drag them somewhere.

Its one network request is the update check: once a day it asks GitHub for the latest release, sending nothing about you. Turn that off in Settings. It doesn't need Accessibility or Input Monitoring.

Screen Recording
To capture and record the screen. It asks only when you click Allow… in the welcome window.
Your Desktop folder
macOS asks once, before Margin first saves into ~/Desktop/Screenshots. Pick another folder in Settings if you like.
Screenshot shortcuts
macOS keeps ⇧⌘3, ⇧⌘4 and ⇧⌘5 until they're turned off. Turn off for me does that when you click it, and Settings can restore them.
The microphone
Only if you turn on Record microphone for screen recordings.
Launch at login
On by default, so the shortcuts work after a restart. Turn it off in Settings.
